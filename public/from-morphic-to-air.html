Origin

From Morphic to AIR

Original story June 2026 · current terminology notes August 2026

AIR did not arrive fully formed. The current framework grew through several earlier attempts to answer the same practical question: how do you make a general model behave like a disciplined resource for sustained work without turning “act as an expert” into the whole architecture?

Morphic

The earliest form was Morphic. Instead of asking the user to pick one permanent expert persona, Morphic selected a role around the task at hand and moved through unrelated tasks one by one. The part that survived is not the role-play framing; it is the instinct that the task should determine the capability brought to it.

The two-site period

For a time the plan split marketing and technical identity between Maugos and VM4AI. That eventually collapsed back into VM4AI as the technical home. The naming changed; the underlying project remained an attempt to make AI work more structured and reusable.

The environment optimizer

An intermediate vm4ai.com tool let users select a model, reasoning “shape,” and modules to generate an overlay. It was useful, but it also exposed the weakness that later became central to AIR: sophisticated language can sound like enforcement even when there is no independent mechanism behind it.

A large part of AIR’s evolution is the decision to keep those claim boundaries explicit rather than letting the interface imply stronger guarantees than the system can support.

The digital teammate experiment

The next iteration used real occupational profiles from sources such as ESCO and O*NET to ground specialized digital teammates. That was more disciplined than a generic “act as” prompt, but it also carried human-career attributes that were irrelevant to the machine’s immediate task.

The turn toward machine-oriented capability

The important realization was to stop treating a human job description as the unit of AI capability. Current AIR uses bounded Specialists, Domain packages, Method packs, and Executors as task inputs instead. They are selected and bound only when the active work needs them, and none of them become independent authorities.

What survived

  • One task at a time. Morphic’s sequential role-switching became AIR’s explicit sole Orbit 0 execution model.
  • Capability follows the task. Human occupational profiles gave way to bounded machine-oriented capability packages.
  • Project continuity matters more than session continuity. Early “passport” experiments became the structured Handoff Card.
  • Structure is not proof. Current AIR separates visible prompt-layer control state from source, tool, operator, and backend evidence.

What changed most

The framework makes narrower public claims than its ancestors. AIR 2.4.3 now formalizes artifact authority, ambiguity handling, active-state reconciliation, action authorization/receipts, evidence classes, reproducibility, and drift recovery. That is a very different posture from simply generating an impressive overlay and trusting the model to inhabit it.

The lineage is useful because the central idea stayed recognizable while the assurance model got stricter: focused work, capability that fits the task, and project state that can move when the container changes.
