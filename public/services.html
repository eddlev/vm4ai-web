Services

Put AIR to work on your actual workflow.

The framework is open. Services are for teams that want help configuring the current runtime, designing bounded capability packages, or establishing a repeatable AIR working model around real projects.

Consulting

Setup & configuration

Configure the current five-file foundation, onboarding posture, working agreement, evidence expectations, and project structure for your environment without weakening the AIR floor.

Enablement

Training & workshops

Hands-on sessions covering onboarding, Orbit 0 binding, gates, evidence, task switching, Handoff, and the difference between prompt-layer records and independently observed evidence.

Capability

Custom package design

Design and validate bounded Specialists, Domain packages, Method packs, and Executors for the work your team actually does. Availability, selection, approval, and binding remain separate states.

Adoption

Workflow & integration design

Map AIR’s portable contract onto host-specific tools, permissions, CI, connectors, or gateways while keeping those stronger mechanical guarantees correctly attributed to the integration.

Review

Governance & evidence posture

Review how project approvals, test evidence, source rights, external actions, and receiver-facing claims are being represented against the current framework boundaries.

Reliability

Support arrangements

Direct support for teams using AIR in sustained workflows. Scope, response expectations, and any service-level commitment are agreed separately from the open-source framework itself.

Important boundary

A service engagement can add implementation support or stronger host-side controls. It does not turn the public prompt-only kit into deterministic backend enforcement unless that infrastructure actually exists and is evidenced.
