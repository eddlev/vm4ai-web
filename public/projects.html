These components do not yet form a production-deployed integrated stack, and each has a different release and licensing history.

Public · Apache-2.0

AIR Kit

Prompt-based project runtime for scope, decisions, evidence, approval and cross-session continuity. The public kit runs inside a compatible AI host; it does not independently enforce external effects.

Public repository and current release ↗
Private development · planned proprietary release

SLUSE

Mandatory interception and enforcement gate for protected agent actions. The AIR backend is the sole authority for ALLOW, REJECT or ESCALATE; SLUSE holds, relays and enforces authentic decisions, without originating a competing verdict.

Local proof-of-concept tests do not establish a production host-enforced deployment.

Research · version-specific licensing

BinLing

Capsule and payload transport concepts alongside experimental deterministic virtual-machine research. Its intended role in the SLUSE architecture is transport only, not authorization.

Native BinLing wire integration is not yet present in the live SLUSE proof of concept.

Architecture boundaries

Decide. Mediate. Transport.

Do not confuse the public prompt-based AIR Kit with the AIR backend service under integration.

AIR backend

Decision

Evaluates authenticated identity, delegated permissions, policy, legal obligations and relevant evidence; issues governance outcomes.

SLUSE

Enforcement

Intercepts protected operations and blocks unauthorized or invalid execution paths.

BinLing

Transport

Serializes and carries payload data without holding authorization authority.

Engineering stage

These are target roles, not a claim of independently verified production enforcement, live native capsule transport or broad IAM interoperability.
