Use cases

Structure that travels with the work.

AIR is domain-agnostic at the core. The task, sources, specialist capability, method, evidence, and approval posture can change; the one-active-task and continuity model stays recognizable.

Code

Development

Research, build, debug, review, and release against one current task instead of letting the session widen silently. AIR keeps scope, source state, acceptance criteria, and consequential actions explicit.

Research

Research & analysis

Separate user intent from externally verifiable claims, keep source-dependent conclusions tied to evidence, and preserve unresolved uncertainty instead of turning it into a convenient fact.

Writing

Writing & documentation

Carry brief, structure, tone requirements, source constraints, review state, and the next active section across sessions without rebuilding the project frame each time.

Creative

Narrative work

When Q4 selects creative narrative continuity, AIR can preserve fictional world rules, character state, timeline, voice constraints, relationships between fictional characters, and unresolved threads while keeping required governance visible.

Brand

Brand & marketing

Keep positioning, voice, evidence boundaries, approvals, and asset decisions coherent across copy, visual systems, campaigns, websites, and handoffs. AIR’s current brand and site are themselves an ongoing self-applied case.

Strategy

Operations & planning

Hold one decision or execution task in Orbit 0 while adjacent risks, dependencies, queued work, and future tasks remain visible without becoming parallel execution.

Capability without surrendering authority

Bring in the missing skill. Keep the project yours.

AIR can bind a Specialist, Domain package, Method pack, or Executor when the task needs capability you do not have in the room. Those components remain bounded inputs to the current artifact—not autonomous agents and not independent sources of authority.

AIR can

Recommend, challenge, and execute within scope

It can surface blind spots, propose a better route, request missing evidence, and perform allowed work against the current contract.

The user keeps

Intent, approval, correction, and irreversible decisions

Approval of a direction does not silently become approval to mutate, publish, deploy, bind, or perform another adjacent material action.
