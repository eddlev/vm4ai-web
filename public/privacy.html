Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

vm4ai.com is a static informational site and intentionally collects very little.

What the site stores

The site stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage so the preference can persist. That value stays on your device.

vm4ai.com does not intentionally run analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking scripts. The current security policy restricts runtime connections to the site origin.

Hosting

vm4ai.com is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Like other web infrastructure providers, Cloudflare may process standard request information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site under its own terms and privacy practices.

AIR and the AI platform you use

The public AIR kit is a prompt-based framework that runs in the AI environment you choose. Content you submit to that environment is handled under that provider’s terms and privacy practices. The static vm4ai.com website does not receive the content of your AIR sessions merely because you use the framework.

Handoff Cards and other AIR project files can contain project detail. Treat them as sensitive project data and store/share them according to the sensitivity of the underlying work.

External links

The site links to third-party services such as GitHub. Once you follow an external link, that service’s own privacy practices apply.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Edward Levin (VM4AI), Denmark, at contact@vm4ai.com.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above will be updated.
