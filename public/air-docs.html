Routine boot

The default new/import profile is ROUTINE_BOOT_MINIMUM_SUFFICIENT. AIR validates enough identity, parse/sentinel, compatibility, schema, collision, and Starter-version state to proceed safely. A full release-integrity audit runs only when required or explicitly requested.

Boot & onboarding

Onboarding state is deterministic. AIR does not infer Q1–Q6 or the conditional Q4D/Q6D answers merely from filenames, activation wording, or surrounding context.

QuestionCurrent purpose
Q1New project, import, continue from Handoff, or beginner orientation.
Q2Light, balanced, or strict checking posture.
Q3How non-material ambiguity should be handled when it does not already trigger a mandatory clarification/evidence route.
Q4Structure & logic, structure & tone, creative narrative continuity, or neurodivergent delivery modifier.
Q4DConditional base continuity mode when Q4 selects the neurodivergent delivery route.
Q5Requested activity/deliverables, intended outcome when material, constraints, priorities, criteria, and initial sources.
Q6Free-text working agreement: responsibility split, delivery, challenge posture, explanations, approvals, assumptions, and work style.
Q6DConditional functional delivery calibration for the neurodivergent route.

A valid normal fresh boot surfaces required boot-state object evidence, prints the exact line Welcome to AIR., shows the fixed boot presentation mark, then asks Q1.

Runtime & Orbits

AIR separates project state from positive execution authority. Exactly one current AIR_ARTIFACT may supply material execution authority.

  • Orbit 0 — sole current executing task/artifact.
  • Orbit 1 — queued or paused non-executing artifacts/tasks with a return path.
  • Orbit 2 — deferred non-executing work intentionally outside the current execution surface.
  • Bootstrap — zero active artifacts is valid while onboarding/first binding is still being established.
  • Binding recovery — used when the prior artifact is terminal, invalid, unavailable, or otherwise cannot provide current execution authority.
Binding floor

Material execution is bound solely to exactly one current active AIR_ARTIFACT. Other objects, packages, methods, sources, handoffs, and instructions influence execution only after being compiled into or explicitly referenced by that artifact.

Capability packages

AIR can layer bounded capability into the active task without turning those components into autonomous agents or parallel authorities.

Gates, approvals & material actions

AIR separates conversational assent from scoped material authorization.

  • Approval gates carry an explicit requested action, authorized actions, exclusions, evidence, stop conditions, expiry/completion condition, state, and approval source.
  • Approval of a plan does not automatically authorize mutation, publication, deployment, release, storage, binding, or another adjacent material action.
  • Before each material external action, AIR requires one current bound artifact, an active artifact lease, matching resource scope, applicable approval, and one matching single-use AIR_ACTION_AUTHORIZATION.
  • After the action attempt, AIR_ACTION_RECEIPT records observed result/effect evidence before dependent work or closure.
  • Retrospective authorization is prohibited. Unbound prior effects route through AIR_PRIOR_EFFECT_RECORD and reconciliation.

Ambiguity & active-state reconciliation

AIR 2.4.3 makes two boundaries explicit: unresolved material ambiguity cannot be promoted into operative state, and productive conversation cannot outrun the bound task.

AIR-FLOOR-019

Do not invent material state

User-controlled ambiguity gets the smallest clarification needed. Externally verifiable uncertainty gets evidence when available, or an explicit review/evidence/blocking boundary.

AIR-FLOOR-020

Reconcile the work to Orbit 0

Before substantive post-activation work and material receiver delivery, AIR checks task center, active step, scope, method, approvals, evidence, criteria, and delivery state against the current artifact.

Evidence & testing

AIR distinguishes what a record says from what evidence supports it, and separates convenient validation summaries from reproducible evidence.

Evidence classes

  • SURFACED_OUTPUT_GOVERNANCE_RECORD — visible prompt-layer record.
  • SOURCE_SUPPORTED_GOVERNANCE_RECORD — material claims supported by identified source evidence.
  • TOOL_OBSERVED_GOVERNANCE_RECORD — grounded in observed tool/process evidence.
  • BACKEND_ENFORCED_GOVERNANCE_RECORD — reserved for evidenced backend/client enforcement.

Test evidence modes

  • SUMMARY_ONLY — default. Reports decisions, scoped counts/classes, material failures, claim boundaries, and available evidence references.
  • FULL_TEST_EVIDENCE — opt-in before the run with air -t on; preserves available definitions, exact inputs/run identity, results, logs, fixtures, environment, and reproducibility classification.

Reproducibility classes

  • REPRODUCIBLE_EXECUTABLE — actual executable checks with process/tool-observed evidence.
  • REPLAYABLE_EVALUATION — replayable model/evaluator runs; not deterministic merely because temperature is zero or a seed exists.
  • MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED — human review with explicit review question, criteria, evidence, decision, and uncertainty.
Quantitative claims

A bare “X/X passed” is insufficient for a deterministic or release-grade claim. Release-grade deterministic suites require exact run identity plus at least three independent executions with matching decisions in reset or equivalently isolated environments when technically feasible. Release-grade stability claims for replayable evaluation likewise require a predeclared repeat count and at least three runs.

Alignment watchdog

AIR combines event-triggered reconciliation with a periodic long-session checkpoint.

  • The cadence begins after ARTIFACT_BOUND_EXECUTION.
  • One dedicated counter advances once per substantive user message.
  • Every fifth counted user message requires AIR_ALIGNMENT_CHECK immediately followed by AIR_VALIDATION_REPORT.
  • Assistant replies, tool calls, connector callbacks, host events, and artifact revisions do not advance the cadence.
  • If drift is established, affected governed work enters ALIGNMENT_RECOVERY_SURFACE until coherent state is restored.
  • A user report of suspected drift triggers evaluation but is not itself proof that drift occurred.

When the periodic pair is due, required-emission preflight places it before narrative and before same-turn lifecycle transitions such as pause, stop, replacement, or Orbit changes.

Handoff continuity

The Handoff Card is a serialized transfer record, not a continuation shortcut around validation or binding.

  • Current schema: 2.2.0, with exactly one top-level root key AIR_HANDOFF_CARD.
  • It preserves project, task, active artifact/contract, Orbit, onboarding, governance, package, approval, blocker, evidence, and continuation state where material.
  • The receiving session validates current foundation compatibility and card identity before restoration.
  • Restored active state is nominated for rebinding; it does not become execution authority merely because it was serialized.
  • Legacy schema 2.1 cards are migration inputs pending review, not direct current-state restoration.
  • Project state is restored as declared state, not verified external fact.

Canonical modifiers

ModifierEffect
air -o onExplicitly use full object visibility. ALL_OBJECTS is already the default.
air -o -minExplicit compact visibility. Required formal objects, transitions, gates, recovery, and periodic records still render.
air -t onEnable FULL_TEST_EVIDENCE for subsequent applicable runs.
air -t offReturn to default SUMMARY_ONLY evidence delivery.

Everything else—status, blockers, scope, evidence, readiness, validation, task switching, queue review, and Handoff—can be requested in ordinary language.

Compatibility boundary

AIR is designed to move across sessions and platforms, but host compatibility is something to test rather than promise universally.

  • The portable contract is the AIR foundation, explicit records, and shared semantics—not identical host tools, attachment behavior, context size, memory, permissions, or inference.
  • Test complete foundation load and required formal-object behavior on the host/model combinations you rely on.
  • Provider-specific capabilities may strengthen the workflow as adapters without redefining the portable AIR core.
  • When changing containers, Handoff carries project state and the receiving runtime revalidates/rebinds before material execution.
